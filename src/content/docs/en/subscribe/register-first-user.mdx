Welcome to the Tesoro CRM registration process! In this guide, we will walk you through the steps to create your account. Tesoro CRM is a powerful platform for real estate professionals, and proper registration is the first step toward an efficient workflow.

:::caution[Important for teams]
The first person to register automatically becomes the Admin and initializes the system for the entire company. Colleagues should **not** start a new registration themselves but must be invited by the Admin.
:::

:::note[Note]
If the registration is not fully completed before entering the verification code, the process must be restarted. After entering the verification code, the process can be resumed via "Forgot Password."
:::

## 1. Starting the Registration

When you visit the Tesoro CRM registration page, you’ll see a clear screen divided into two parts:

- Left: the registration form
- Right: information about Tesoro CRM

Click the "Register your account" button to begin.

## 2. Personal Information

In this step, you’ll fill in your personal details:

1. First name
2. Last name
3. Email address

Carefully review your information and click "Next" to proceed.

## 3. Email Verification

After submitting your details, you’ll receive a verification email:

1. Open your email inbox
2. Look for an email from Tesoro CRM
3. Find the 6-digit verification code
4. Enter the code into the six boxes on the screen

## 5. Company Information

As the first user, you need to enter your company details:

:::danger[Note]
Enter the company name exactly as you want it to appear. This name will be used throughout the system and is crucial for your organization’s recognition.
:::

Fill in the company name and click "Next" to continue.

## 6. Role Selection

As the first user, you’ll automatically be assigned the Admin role. This gives you full control over the system, including the ability to:

- Invite new users
- Manage system settings
- Update company details

Select your role and click "Next."

If you abandon the registration at this stage, you’ll need to start the entire process over. Ensure you have time to complete the registration.
:::

## 4. Set Password

Choose a strong password that meets the following requirements:

- Minimum of 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one special character
- At least one number

You can make the password visible using the eye icon to avoid typos.

## Common Issues and Solutions

### Verification Code Not Received?

1. Check your spam folder
2. Wait a few minutes
3. Click "Resend code"
4. Verify that your email address was entered correctly

### Registration Interrupted?

- **Before verification**: Restart the entire process
- **After verification**: Use "Forgot Password" to continue

### Registering Colleagues

:::danger[Warning]
Colleagues should **NOT** start a new registration themselves. This would create a new company account, which is not the intended outcome.

✅ Correct approach:

1. The Admin invites new users
2. Users receive an invitation link
3. Users complete their registration via the invitation

❌ Incorrect approach:

- Registering independently
- Creating a new company account
:::

## Conclusion

- ✅ The first user automatically becomes the Admin
- ✅ Colleagues are invited by the Admin
- ✅ Complete the registration in one session
- ✅ Store your login credentials securely

After registration, you can immediately begin setting up your Tesoro CRM environment. Follow the instructions in the dashboard for the next steps.
