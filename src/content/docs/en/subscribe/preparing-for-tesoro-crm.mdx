## Step 1: Define Your Goals and Expectations

Before you start the transition, it's important to have a clear understanding of what you want to achieve with Tesoro CRM:

- What challenges do you want to solve?
- Which processes do you want to streamline?
- What reports are essential for your business operations?
- What are your expectations for the user experience?

## Step 2: Map Out Your Team and Processes

### Team and Roles

:::caution
Ensure there is always at least one Admin to manage the system.
:::

Tesoro CRM has two roles:

**Admin**: Has the highest permissions and can configure company settings, create and invite users.

**Employee**: Has the same rights as an admin except for settings, but cannot modify company settings or manage users.

### Current Workflow

**Current Systems:**
- What CRM software are you currently using?
- Do you use separate systems for email, calendar, or document management?
- Do you work with specific real estate software or portals?

**Work Processes:**
- Map out your current workflows:
  - Lead management (how are leads followed up?)
  - Deal flow (steps from lead to closing)
  - Reporting (what data do you need?)
  - Document management (where are documents stored?)

### Real Estate Portals

Create an overview of all portals you're active on, such as:
- Idealista (via API)
- Fotocasa (future integration)
- Other relevant platforms

For each portal, we need:
- Portal name
- ILC code (only for Idealista)
- Portal contact person (name, email, phone)
- Current API keys or login credentials
- Specific wishes or requirements for the integration

*Note:* For some portals (like Idealista), we need to officially notify them about the switch to Tesoro CRM. Keep this in mind for your planning.

### Data to Migrate

:::

- Customer and contact information

### Current CRM Information

For a smooth migration, we need:
- Name and version of your current CRM system
- Available export options (CSV, Excel, API)
- How your data is currently organized
- Important relationships between different types of data

### Data Structure in Tesoro CRM

In Tesoro, contacts are managed in a single database and can be classified as either Lead or Contact:

- **Leads**: Enter through the lead interface and are treated as leads.
- **Contacts**: Once a lead is converted to a deal and a contact, the classification changes to Contact.

### Import Instructions

:::caution
**Important Notice About Data Import**

Importing data is often more complex than it seems. Due to differences in data structures between systems, issues may arise such as:

- Incorrect field mapping
- Data loss
- Duplicate or incomplete records
- Problems with special characters or formatting

We strongly recommend contacting your Tesoro CRM Success Manager before starting the import. They can guide you through the process and ensure your data is transferred correctly.
:::

You can import data into Tesoro CRM in several ways, depending on the type of data:

1. **Importing Leads**
   - Go to: [Import Leads](https://crm.tesoro.estate/leads/import)
   - Or click the button in the top right of the Leads overview and choose "Import"

2. **Importing Relations**
   - Go to: [Import Relations](https://crm.tesoro.estate/relations/import)
   - Or click the button in the top right of the Relations overview and choose "Import"

3. **Importing Contacts**
   - Go to: [Import Contacts](https://crm.tesoro.estate/contacts/import)
   - Or click the button in the top right of the Contacts overview and choose "Import"

4. **Importing Deals** (coming soon)
   - Will be added soon

In this screen, you'll map the columns from your CSV file to the corresponding fields in Tesoro CRM:
- **Left column**: Shows the fields in your CSV file
- **Right column**: Shows the corresponding fields in Tesoro CRM
- **Map**: Drag fields from left to right or use the arrows to connect them
- **Advanced options**: Click the gear icon for additional settings
- **Complete**: Click "Next" to start the import

*Tip: Save your field mappings as a profile to reuse them later!*

Schedule a training session for your team to familiarize them with Tesoro CRM. Focus on:
