In this section, you will find all the information about the various subscriptions and pricing plans for Tesoro.

## Subscribing to Tesoro CRM

Real estate agents can easily sign up for Tesoro CRM, as previously discussed in the registration section. Every newly registered agent receives 7 days of free access to a fully functional version of Tesoro CRM. There is no difference between the paid and trial versions.

### 1. Free Trial Period

- **Duration**: 7 days
- **Functionality**: Identical to the paid version
- **Limitation**: After 7 days, access to Tesoro CRM will be blocked until payment details are provided and the first payment is completed.
- **Data retention**: If no payment details are entered, the account remains active, and all entered data is preserved. Once payment is completed, access is restored, and you can continue working from where you left off.

### 2. Payment and Packages

- **Price**: €99 per month
- **Included**: Up to 10 users
- **More than 10 users**: Custom pricing, contact Tesoro Sales

### 3. Payment Process

- **Payment method**: Credit card (via Stripe)
- **Where to find it**:
  1. Go to Company Settings as an Admin
  2. Click on Subscriptions & Plans
  3. Select the Default Plan
  4. Follow the payment instructions

### 4. Cancellation and Subscription Duration

- **Prepayment**: Subscriptions are always paid in advance.
- **Monthly subscription**: If canceled, the subscription remains active until the end of the paid month.
- **Annual subscription**: If canceled, the subscription remains active until the end of the paid year.
- **Changes in subscription type**: If a user switches from a monthly to an annual subscription (or vice versa), the new subscription period takes effect only after the current payment term ends.

### 5. Visualizations

For a smooth onboarding experience, images will be added of:

- The settings page where subscriptions can be managed
- The subscription selection screen

After successful payment, the subscription is activated immediately, and the CRM remains fully accessible.
